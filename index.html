Who is Intrinsic for?

Designed for DIY value investors, finance students, and portfolio managers who are tired of broken spreadsheets. If you want institutional-grade DCF models without the Bloomberg Terminal price tag, this is for you.

Privacy by Design

We believe your financial data is yours. Intrinsic is a Local-First application. Your watchlists, calculations, and API keys are stored securely on your Mac. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Bring Your Own Key

Instead of charging you expensive monthly subscriptions for data, Intrinsic uses a BYOK model. You plug in your own free Finnhub and Google Gemini API keys directly in the settings for unlimited use.

1

Enter Ticker

Type any US stock ticker. Intrinsic instantly fetches live financials, cash flows, and debt metrics via Finnhub.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Intrinsic.

Is the market data real-time?

Intrinsic fetches end-of-day data for most metrics and real-time quotes when available via standard public financial APIs. It's designed for long-term fundamental investors, not high-frequency day traders.

Which stock markets are supported?

We currently support major global exchanges including NYSE and NASDAQ. Simply type the ticker.

Is my financial data private?

Yes, absolutely. All calculations and assumption inputs happen locally on your Mac. We do not track your portfolio, your watchlists, or your API keys. You are in total control.

Do I need to be a finance expert to use it?

No. While the tools are professional-grade (DCF, PEG, Monte Carlo), the interface is designed to be highly intuitive. We provide helpful tooltips and context for every financial input.
